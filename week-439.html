Bonus Ball Pre-Draw View

A premium favorite with the cleanest projected route is still the safest multiplier framework, even if the final call should wait for the official bracket.

When aggression can work

A more aggressive Bonus Ball becomes viable when a non-premium player lands in a genuinely open section and carries the scoring profile to capitalize quickly.

What to avoid
